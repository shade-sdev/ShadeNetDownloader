ShadeNet Downloader: third-party software
==========================================

ShadeNet Downloader ships with, and uses, the programs and libraries below. Each keeps its own licence.

Programs that are started by ShadeNet (they are separate programs, not linked into it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

yt-dlp
    Finds the video files behind web pages such as YouTube.
    https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
    Licence: The Unlicense (public domain dedication).

FFmpeg (LGPL build)
    Joins downloaded video and audio into one file without re-encoding.
    https://ffmpeg.org
    Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. The licence text is in tools\ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt
    next to the program. This build is the LGPL variant published by https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds ;
    its source code and build scripts are available from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/download.html#get-sources) and that
    project. FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
    You can replace this ffmpeg with another build at any time: Settings > Downloads > Helper programs.
